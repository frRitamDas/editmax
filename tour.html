How it works

Three steps from raw clip to live ad

No timeline scrubbing. No template hunting. Give EditMax the product and the angle, and it does the boring 40 minutes.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to be a video editor?
No. You bring footage and a product. EditMax handles timing, captions, pacing and export sizes. Most sellers ship their first ad in under ten minutes.
What footage does it work with?
Phone clips, product stills, screen recordings and existing ads. Upload MP4, MOV, PNG or JPG up to 4K. If you only have product photos, EditMax can build a motion cut from them.
Will it look like every other template ad?
EditMax is trained on your brand kit and your own past winners, not a shared template library. Hooks and pacing adapt to what your account's audience actually finishes.
How does the free trial work?
You pick a plan, add a card for verification, and get 3 days free. You're charged on day 3 unless you cancel. Cancel from your account page in two clicks — no email required.
Can I export to TikTok Shop directly?
Every render drops into a library as 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9, safe-area aware, ready to upload to TikTok Shop, Ads Manager or your organic account.
What if I don't like the cut?
Ask for a change in plain English — “tighter hook”, “less text”, “slower” — and it re-cuts. Edits don't count twice against your monthly limit.
