GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick
answers.

EditMax is intentionally small and transparent. Here's what this browser-based version can—and can't—do.

Does my video get uploaded?

No. Your file is opened locally by your browser. The editor doesn't send your video anywhere, create an account, or store your media.

What does the editor do?

This frontend lets you preview a local video, set a start and end time, and record that selection as a downloadable clip. It is a focused trimming tool, not a full multitrack or AI editing suite.

Why does export take as long as the clip?

To keep processing on your device without a server, the browser records the selected section while it plays. Keep this tab open and the video unpaused until the download begins.

Which browsers can export?

Export is available where MediaRecorder and video capture are supported. Chrome, Edge and Firefox on desktop are recommended. Preview and trim controls still work if a browser can't export.
